## 📌 Version Highlights

### 1.6.0_spatial_3d_webar_mandapam (Latest)
- **Multi-Surface Projection:** Switch seamlessly between **Table / Floor Mandapam** (grounded with Rangoli shadow base), **360° Free Orbit** (inspect from any angle), and **Wall Shrine** (vertical wall projection).
- **Sculpted 3D Architecture:** 3D tiered temple Gopuram with Kalasham spires, carved golden side pillars, and marble pedestal with flickering brass Diyas.
- **Interactive 3D Temple Bell:** Hanging brass bell swings on tap and chimes with a flower petal explosion.
- **3D Marigold & Rose Petal Physics:** Real-time canvas particle simulation of falling marigold and rose petals in AR.
- **Gesture Controls:** 1-finger rotate, 2-finger pinch-to-zoom, and 2-finger pan translation.
